## 4. Key Findings

- Scenario-clustered CIs are on average **1.71x wider** than episode-level CIs (range: 1.65x–1.74x).
- This confirms **positive intra-scenario correlation**: episodes from the same scenario are not exchangeable, validating the choice of scenario-clustered resampling as the statistically conservative approach.
- Friedman test p=0.2046 is not significant; bootstrap CI [0.0002, 0.8507] spans 0.05 — interpret with caution.
