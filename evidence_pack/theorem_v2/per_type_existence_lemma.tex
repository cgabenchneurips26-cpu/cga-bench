\subsection{Per-coordinate separating-pair existence (refinement of Lemma~\ref{lem:separating-pairs})}
\label{app:per-type-existence}

The separating-pair existence claim of
Lemma~\ref{lem:separating-pairs} can be refined into a coordinate-
specific existence map by appeal to the empirical estimator
$\widehat{\varepsilon}^{\star,k}_{\pi}$ of
Corollary~\ref{cor:empirical-bayes} applied to the per-coordinate
verdict $V_{G,k}$. The estimator is strictly positive on a finite
sample \emph{iff} at least one verdict-heterogeneous $\pi$-fibre
exists in the sample, which in turn implies the population-level
existence of a separating pair for coordinate $k$ under projection $\pi$.

\begin{lemma}[Per-coordinate existence map]
\label{lem:per-type-existence}
Fix a guideline $G$ admissible in CGA-Bench and a finite sample
$\widehat{D}\subseteq\mathcal{T}$. For each trace projection
$\pi\in\{\pi_{\mathrm{term}},\pi_{\mathrm{aset}},\pi_{\mathrm{nord}},\pi_{\mathrm{nctx}}\}$
and each violation coordinate
$k\in\{\textsc{omit},\textsc{commit},\textsc{time},\textsc{seq},\textsc{dev}\}$,
if $\widehat{\varepsilon}^{\star,k}_{\pi}(G,\widehat{D})>0$ then there
exists a pair of traces $(\tau_1,\tau_2)\in\widehat{D}$ with
$\pi(\tau_1)=\pi(\tau_2)$ and
$V_{G,k}(\tau_1)\neq V_{G,k}(\tau_2)$.
The converse also holds: if no such pair exists in $\widehat{D}$, then
$\widehat{\varepsilon}^{\star,k}_{\pi}(G,\widehat{D})=0$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Forward direction: by definition of the plug-in estimator
(App.~\ref{app:thm-empirical}),
$\widehat{\varepsilon}^{\star,k}_{\pi}(G,\widehat{D})>0$ requires at
least one fibre $y$ with $\min_{v}|\{i:V_{G,k}(\tau^{(i)})=v,\pi(\tau^{(i)})=y\}|\geq 1$,
which means the fibre contains at least two traces
$\tau_1,\tau_2$ with $V_{G,k}(\tau_1)\neq V_{G,k}(\tau_2)$.
Converse: if every fibre is verdict-pure in coordinate $k$, every
per-fibre count contribution to the estimator is zero. \hfill $\square$
\end{proof}

(\textit{\checkmark}) indicates an empirical separating pair exists in

Two structural observations follow immediately.

\paragraph{Terminal and action-set projections are empirically blind to
every coordinate.} Rows $\pi_{\mathrm{term}}$ and $\pi_{\mathrm{aset}}$
are fully checked: there exists at least one trajectory pair in the
CGA-Bench public release that an evaluator restricted to those
projections cannot tell apart on \emph{any} of the five violation
coordinates. This is the strongest form of the paper's
``structurally incomplete'' claim for these two projections.

\paragraph{Ordered projections resolve timing and sequence
empirically.} Rows $\pi_{\mathrm{nord}}$ and $\pi_{\mathrm{nctx}}$
show no separating pair for \textsc{time} or \textsc{seq} in the
corpus. Theorem~\ref{thm:coarsening}'s strict positivity for these
two projections (the aggregated $\widehat{\varepsilon}^{\star}_{\pi}>0$
in Corollary~\ref{cor:empirical-bayes}) is therefore carried
entirely by the \textsc{omit}, \textsc{commit}, and \textsc{dev}
coordinates. A reviewer-skeptical reading would note that this is a
\emph{corpus property}: CGA-Bench's simulator places actions at
deterministic 5-minute intervals, so an agent's action-sequence fully
determines timing under $\pi_{\mathrm{nctx}}$ with 5-minute binning.
Coarser binning or a stochastic simulator would populate the
\textsc{time} cells. The theorem's existence claim is preserved
along the remaining three coordinates; no coordinate is empirically
resolved under $\pi_{\mathrm{term}}$ or $\pi_{\mathrm{aset}}$, so
the aggregated bound is tight where it matters for process-
oblivious evaluators.
