\subsection{Per-coordinate Bayes error}
\label{app:per-type-bayes}

Table~\ref{tab:per-type-bayes} decomposes the Bayes error of
Theorem~\ref{thm:coarsening} along the five violation coordinates
$k\in\{\textsc{omit},\textsc{commit},\textsc{time},\textsc{seq},\textsc{dev}\}$.
Each cell is the plug-in estimator
$\widehat{\varepsilon}^{\star,k}_{\pi}(G,D)$ of the Bayes error for the
$k$-th coordinate under projection $\pi$, computed on the same
$N=\bayesErrNEpisodes$ CGA-Bench episodes. Bootstrap 95\% CI uses
$B=200$ resamples per cell.

Three observations tie the per-coordinate table back to the main
theorem.

\paragraph{Terminal-state blindness is driven by timing.}
Under $\pi_{\mathrm{term}}$, the timing coordinate hits
$\widehat{\varepsilon}^{\star,\textsc{time}}_{\mathrm{term}}=\bayesErrCoordTermTime$,
which is the dominant contribution to the aggregated
$\widehat{\varepsilon}^{\star}_{\mathrm{term}}=\bayesErrTerm$. Terminal-
output-only evaluators (TOM, discharge-diagnosis match) must
misclassify roughly $43\%$ of timing-violation verdicts on the CGA-
Bench distribution.

\paragraph{Action-set coarsening blinds against omissions.}
$\widehat{\varepsilon}^{\star,\textsc{omit}}_{\mathrm{aset}}=\bayesErrCoordAsetOmit$
is $4.5\times$ the aggregated $\pi_{\mathrm{aset}}$ Bayes error
($\bayesErrAset$). ASC, PAF, and MAB-F1 share the $\pi_{\mathrm{aset}}$
coarsening and must misclassify at least $\sim 11\%$ of omission
verdicts. This matches E1's orthogonal-perturbation finding that
action-set evaluators miss \textsc{must}-omit violations that leave
the multiset unchanged.

\paragraph{Ordered-action projections resolve timing and sequence.}
$\widehat{\varepsilon}^{\star,\textsc{time}}_{\mathrm{nord}}=\widehat{\varepsilon}^{\star,\textsc{seq}}_{\mathrm{nord}}=0$
on this corpus under 5-minute timestamp binning. The projections
$\pi_{\mathrm{nord}}$ and $\pi_{\mathrm{nctx}}$ therefore preserve
enough information to disambiguate every timing and sequence
violation instance present in CGA-Bench. Theorem~\ref{thm:coarsening}'s
strict-positivity for these projections is carried entirely by the
omission and deviation coordinates ($\widehat{\varepsilon}>0$),
reflecting the corpus finding in Corollary~\ref{cor:empirical-bayes}.
