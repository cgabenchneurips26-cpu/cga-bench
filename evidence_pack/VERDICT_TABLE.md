## Honest Limitations

1. **Cross-comparison is metric-proxy, not exact reproduction**: CGA's "Task Completion" = C2 >= 1.0, not identical to original benchmark metrics (MedAgentBench SR, AgentClinic Dx accuracy)
2. **MedChain 99.8% general fallback**: most episodes evaluated against universal_clinical_safety.yaml, not domain-specific CPG
3. **Perturbation is sensitivity analysis**: synthetic baselines, not real LLM behavior
4. **Sequence FM structurally absent from Q2**: pure sequence violations never occur alone (always with omission)
5. **HealthBench keyword classifier has ~73% false positive rate**: "give" matches "given", "order" matches "disorder"
6. **C3 scoring fix (2026-03-31)**: C3 (forbidden avoidance) was returning 1.0 when no forbidden actions existed in the current CPG node, masking commission violations against the full forbidden set. Fix lowered CGA scores by ~0.007 for 3 models and shifted Composite A single-run from p=0.043 to p=0.073 (no longer significant). Multi-run means (p=0.020) remain significant.
